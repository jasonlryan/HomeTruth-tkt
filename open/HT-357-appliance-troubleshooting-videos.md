# HT-357 · Offer a trusted troubleshooting video for a specific appliance issue

**Repo:** both
**Priority:** P2
**Created:** 2026-11-19
**Status:** Open · follow-up to [HT-356](HT-356-ask-appliance-manual-handoff.md)

## Goal

When a homeowner describes a symptom or error code, answer from the applicable manual and offer a relevant video if a trustworthy source demonstrates the same issue. Looking for a manual alone does not trigger a video search.

## Implementation and acceptance

- Prefer the manufacturer’s official support page or verified manufacturer YouTube channel. For an independent fallback, require a named, reviewable source with relevant appliance expertise, an applicable model or documented model family, and steps consistent with official instructions. Popularity and channel branding alone are insufficient.
- Show title, publisher, link, issue and model applicability, why it was selected and whether it is manufacturer-produced or independent. Record the source and date checked. Give a timestamp only when the segment has been verified. A channel-only suggestion should be labelled as a place to browse.
- Keep the manual and its citations available. If no suitable video is verified, omit the recommendation and offer official support. For an issue the manufacturer reserves for a qualified technician, show that instruction and the support route.
- A public link can be offered without copying a video or claiming its transcript has been indexed. Any proposed paid discovery path needs explicit product approval for its trigger and budget before rollout.

## Verification

Use fixtures for an official exact-model video, a credible independent model-family demonstration, a popular wrong-variant video, a removed video, an issue requiring service and no suitable result. Verify attribution, relevance, honest fallback and zero video discovery calls on a plain manual request.
