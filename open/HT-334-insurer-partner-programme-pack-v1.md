# HT-334: Deliver the insurer partner-programme pack v1

**Priority:** P1
**Repo:** backend / frontend / docs
**Milestone:** B2B2C partner-programme foundation
**Created:** 2026-08-02
**Updated:** 2026-08-02

## Goal

Deliver the first commercial B2B2C pack for an insurer-sponsored homeowner programme using the shared partner-programme core.

## Objective

Provide insurer-appropriate content, document prompts, prevention actions, approved measures and launch materials without introducing insurer control over homeowner records.

## Scope

- Insurance policy, renewal, EPC, survey, service-certificate and maintenance-record prompts.
- Conservative prevention task rules and homeowner-facing explanations.
- Insurer programme measures based on approved aggregate engagement and outcomes.
- Approved co-branded acquisition and pilot review material.
- No individual homeowner data access by default.

## Out Of Scope

- Claims decisions, underwriting, pricing, premium reduction or policy compliance enforcement.
- Insurer access to individual tasks, documents, property facts or chat.
- Mortgage-provider and new-build functionality.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The pack runs on the shared programme, consent and reporting model with no insurer-specific fork.
- [ ] Homeowner content is useful without implying insurer requirements or outcomes.
- [ ] Document prompts and task rules are explainable and have approved sources.
- [ ] Partner evidence is aggregate-only, thresholded and decision-ready.
- [ ] Launch copy states the homeowner/partner data boundary accurately.
- [ ] A target-environment rehearsal covers the insurer programme route end to end.
- [ ] A feature branch, PR and clean review loop are completed.

## Dependencies

- HT-328 scope gate accepted.
- HT-329 through HT-332 as applicable.
- Current HT-320 launch and privacy gates remain independently satisfied for any live cohort.
